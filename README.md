# Regular Show Story Studio

A lightweight single-page app for crafting short films featuring the Regular Show cast.
Design character beats, style their outfits, and assemble scenes into a mini movie. The
park crew welcomes **Sydney**, a brand-new character with a music-tech flair.

## Features

- **Character wardrobe & emotion designer** – Mix emotions, actions, clothing, accessories,
  and shoes for every beat.
- **Scene beat board** – Stack customized beats before saving them into a scene.
- **Film timeline** – Organize scenes, review total durations, and produce a shareable story
  outline.
- **Quick export** – Copy the outline to pitch episodes, shorts, or fan films.

## Getting started

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Use the form on the left to configure a character beat.
3. Add multiple beats to craft a scene, then save it to the film timeline.
4. Export the outline and paste it into your storyboard or production notes.

For local development with live reload, serve the directory with your favorite static server
(e.g., `npx serve`, `python -m http.server`).
