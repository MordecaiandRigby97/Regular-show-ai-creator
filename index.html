Sydney's Story with the Band

Sydney Greenspoon's Regular Show narrated diary explains how she first met the band: Joe Schermie invited her to a rehearsal art jam, Floyd Sneed welcomed her backstage after spotting her sketch of his drum kit, and Chuck Negron shared harmonies over coffee while mapping a charity concert. Those hangouts sealed a creative pact and made her family.

Family Connections

Legacy Timeline

1968 · Formation

Floyd's precision drumming leads to him being recruited for the vocal trio's backing band, sparking Three Dog Night's explosive debut.

1970 · "Mama Told Me" #1

The band wins over pop radio with a swampy groove, propelled by Floyd's loose hi-hat feel.

1973 · Global Tours

Sneed brings his artist's eye to stage designs while juggling nightly drum solos.

2023 · Farewell

The music community celebrates his life, sharing paintings, session notes, and rare film reels for future remasters.
